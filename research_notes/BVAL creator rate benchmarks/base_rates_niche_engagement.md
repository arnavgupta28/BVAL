# BVAL: Sponsored-Content Base Rates, CPMs, Niche Multipliers, Engagement Benchmarks (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-22. All USD unless noted. FX used: **1 USD = ₹93.5** (USD/INR traded 93.27-94.55 in Nov 2026; 93.27 early trade Nov 18 2026 — [HDFC Sky](https://hdfcsky.com/news/rupee-rises-24-paise-to-93-27-against-us-dollar-in-early-trade-november-18-2026); 93.91 on Nov 6 per FRED series — [MarketXLS](https://marketxls.com/indicators/usd-to-inr-exchange-rate); 94.20 close Nov 13 — [HDFC Sky](https://hdfcsky.com/news/rupee-falls-8-paise-to-close-at-94-20-against-us-dollar)).

Source quality note: there is no single authoritative, audited dataset. Most numbers come from vendor or agency blogs (Later, LeadDyno, IMH, Modash, upGrowth, Grynow) that do not publish methodology. Marketplace data (Collabstr) is the closest to "real transaction prices" but its main report pages returned HTTP 403, so I relied on search excerpts. Ranges across sources differ by 2-10x, mostly because tier boundaries and "per post vs per bundle" differ.

## 1. Base price by platform x deliverable x follower tier

### Takeaway
For US/global creators, the sources converge on roughly: nano Reel $100-500, micro Reel $500-2,500, mid $2,500-6,000, macro $6,000-10,000, mega $10,000-50,000+. TikTok runs about 40-50% below Instagram Reels; Stories about 0.5x a static post; carousels 1.25-1.5x static; YouTube integrations cost 50-60% of a dedicated video. IMH's 2026 cheat sheet sits much lower at nano/micro, so treat its numbers as the low end.

## 2. CPM / cost-per-view and per-follower rules; views vs followers

### Takeaway
Typical sponsored CPMs on views run: TikTok $7-25, Instagram Reels $10-45, Stories $20-50, YouTube integration $15-35 (niche-dependent, $6-55), YouTube dedicated $50-75+. The per-follower rule is about $10 per 1K followers for Instagram ($100 per 10K). Pros recommend anchoring on average or expected views, not followers. That supports BVAL's plan to use median recent Reel views.

### Cited Findings
- Modash CPM estimates (US/UK, anecdotal, undated): IG Stories $20-50; IG Reels $15-45; TikTok $7-25; YouTube integration $15-35; YouTube dedicated $50-75+ — [Modash](https://www.modash.io/blog/influencer-marketing-cpm)
- IMH 2026 Instagram guide: influencer CPMs often land between $5 and $25 — [IMH Instagram rates](https://influencermarketinghub.com/instagram-influencer-rates/) (search excerpt)
- iQfluence 2026: practical planning benchmark $10-50 CPM. Reels $12-58 CPM, citing Modash 2026 DACH research — [iQfluence](https://iqfluence.io/public/blog/cpm-influencer-marketing) (search excerpt)
- TrySpansa YouTube 60s-integration CPM by niche: $6-55 (see section 3). Hybrid deals use a CPV benchmark of **$0.018-0.023 per view** (= $18-23 CPM), citing IMH. Aspire 2026 industry-wide average CPM cited as **$2.68**. That number is likely impression-based paid media and very low compared with creator-fee CPMs, so do not use it as a base — [TrySpansa](https://dev.to/tryspansa/influencer-rate-card-what-brands-should-pay-43oj)
- CreatorDB (via AOL, 2025): YouTube influencer rates typically $15-25 CPM in higher cost-of-living countries — [AOL](https://www.aol.com/2025-influencer-marketing-costs-youtube-133000232.html)
- Lumanu (via Buffer): $0.05-0.10 per average video view for YouTube ($50-100 CPM, dedicated-level) — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/influencer-marketing-cost/)
- Per-follower: Buffer gives **~$10 per 1,000 followers** for Instagram (Digiday guideline of ~$1,000 per 100K). A lifestyle blogger's formula is $5-10 per 1K — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/influencer-marketing-cost/)
- Modash cites a "very basic" rule of ~$100 per 1,000 followers but says real rates are often lower. That is 10x Buffer's figure — [Modash](https://www.modash.io/blog/influencer-pricing)
- X/Twitter: ~$2 per 1K followers — [LeadDyno](https://www.leaddyno.com/blog/influencer-pricing)
- Views vs followers: Collabstr calls the per-follower formula weaker than pricing on expected views. Its method is fee ÷ expected views × 1,000, and its worked example comes to ~$9.65 per 1K views (≈$0.01/view). It prices a 50K-follower account at $500/post on the follower rule — [Collabstr influencer pay](https://collabstr.com/blog/influencer-pay) (search excerpt)
- TrySpansa: subscriber count is "the wrong one" to fixate on. Its offer formula is **Niche CPM × (avg views / 1,000) × format multiplier**, and it uses tier rate × niche × format only as a budget sanity range — [TrySpansa](https://dev.to/tryspansa/influencer-rate-card-what-brands-should-pay-43oj)
- HypeAuditor's ER methodology now notes that follower count is no longer a reliable proxy for how many people see content — [HypeAuditor via search](https://hypeauditor.com/digest-august-26/)

### Inferences
- Use views as the primary anchor and followers only as a sanity band. Example config: `price = median_reel_views / 1000 × CPM[platform][deliverable] × niche_mult × er_adj`, then clamp to [0.5x, 2x] of the follower-tier table in section 1. This catches accounts with viral outliers or dead audiences.
- Suggested baseline CPMs (US, general/lifestyle niche, on views):
  - IG Reel $20
  - IG Story frame $25 (applied to story views, which are lower)
  - IG static: use reach ≈ 0.3× followers if views are unavailable, at $20
  - TikTok $12
  - YT Shorts $8
  - YT integration $22
  - YT dedicated $60

  These sit inside the midpoints of the Modash, IMH and TrySpansa ranges.
- Sanity check: a micro creator with 30K median Reel views × $20 = $600. That falls inside the $500-2,500 micro Reel band.
- $10 per 1K followers (Buffer) at 50K followers = $500, consistent with Collabstr. Keep $10 per 1K as the follower-based fallback for Instagram.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 dataset publishes CPM by follower tier.
- No authoritative TikTok creator CPM for 2026 was found. Modash's $7-25 is undated and anecdotal.
- Sources do not consistently say whether the CPM denominator is views, impressions or reach.

## 3. Niche multipliers

### Takeaway
Finance, crypto, B2B/SaaS and real estate are the most expensive (about 2-3x a lifestyle baseline). Tech, education and health sit at 1.2-1.6x. Beauty, fashion, travel and lifestyle are about 1x. Food, parenting and DIY run 0.85-0.9x, entertainment 0.7x, and gaming is cheapest at about 0.5-0.6x. India sources rank beauty higher (a premium over fitness and gaming).

### Cited Findings
- TrySpansa (Apr 2026) YouTube 60s-integration CPM by niche: crypto/finance $20-55; real estate $25-50; business/SaaS $20-45; technology $18-40; education/science $15-35; health $14-32; fitness/sports $14-30; lifestyle, beauty, travel, fashion, auto $12-28; food/cooking, DIY/home, family/parenting $10-25; entertainment/comedy $8-20; music and anime $8-18; ASMR $6-16; gaming $6-15. Built from "15+ industry reports" by the publisher itself (a vendor source) — [TrySpansa](https://dev.to/tryspansa/influencer-rate-card-what-brands-should-pay-43oj)
- Other niche CPM claims disagree: finance & B2B SaaS $50-150 CPM; personal finance $30-60; tech reviews $25-45; beauty $18-35 vs $5-8 (micro); gaming $6-15 vs $8-25. Finance YouTubers command 3-5x gaming at equal subscriber count — [search summary: dev.to finance](https://dev.to/tryspansa/youtube-finance-sponsorship-rates-2026-the-20-55-cpm-tier-57ch); [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-sponsorship-rates/); [InfluenceFlow](https://influenceflow.io/resources/youtube-creator-sponsorship-rates-2025-complete-pricing-guide-for-all-creator-tiers); [dev.to gaming](https://dev.to/tryspansa/youtube-gaming-sponsorship-rates-2026-the-6-15-cpm-niche-13nb)
- LeadDyno 2026 niche groups vs baseline: premium (finance, investing, B2B, SaaS, credentialed health) **2-3x**; above average (home design, pets, travel, parenting) **1.5-2x**; mid (beauty, skincare, fitness) **1-1.5x**; baseline (lifestyle, fashion, food & beverage) **~1x**. Median IG Reel by niche: home design $1,500; pets $1,000; travel $750; beauty $225. Micro finance post midpoint ~$1,250; B2B/SaaS micro Reel ~$1,000. Collabstr-style marketplace averages: education $307; entrepreneurship/business $295; comedy $292 — [LeadDyno](https://www.leaddyno.com/blog/influencer-pricing)
- Influee (via LeadDyno): a micro finance/SaaS creator charges $500-1,500 per Reel versus $150-500 for general lifestyle (≈3x) — [LeadDyno](https://www.leaddyno.com/blog/influencer-pricing)
- India (upGrowth, Sep 2026 estimates): beauty/lifestyle carry a **40-80% premium over fitness and gaming**; finance runs **60-120% above** comparable creators; food and travel are mid-range. Example for a 50K micro creator: beauty/fashion ₹25,000-60,000 vs fitness/food ₹15,000-35,000 — [upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/influencer-marketing-pricing-india-2026/)
- IMH names finance, B2B tech, luxury, healthcare and software as higher-value niches (no numbers) — [IMH](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-rates/)

Suggested niche multipliers relative to lifestyle = 1.0. Derived mainly from TrySpansa CPM midpoints (lifestyle midpoint $20), cross-checked against LeadDyno groups:

|---|---|---|

| business / B2B / SaaS | 1.6 | TrySpansa mid $32.5; LeadDyno 2-3x |
| tech | 1.45 | TrySpansa mid $29 |

### Gaps

## 4. Engagement-rate benchmarks and price adjustments

### Takeaway
On Instagram, HypeAuditor's Nov 2026 view-based ER (likes + comments ÷ views) puts the "average" band at about 2-6% for nano and about 1.7-4.5% for 1M+. Follower-based ER is much lower (HypeAuditor's 2026 report: nano 1.78%, micro 0.54%). TikTok runs higher than Instagram, and YouTube runs lowest (on followers). No published, standardized percentage premium for above-benchmark ER exists. Sources say high ER moves a creator to the top of the tier range.

### Cited Findings
- HypeAuditor Instagram ER benchmarks (2026, data through Nov 2026). Method: (likes + comments) ÷ views if the last 30 posts include ≥3 Reels/videos, else ÷ followers. Low / average / high bands:
  - <10K: <2.28% / 2.28-6.07% / ≥6.07%
  - 10-50K: <2.02% / 2.02-5.37% / ≥5.37%
  - 50-100K: <1.93% / 1.93-5.12% / ≥5.12%
  - 100-500K: <1.90% / 1.90-5.06% / ≥5.06%
  - 500K-1M: <2.00% / 2.00-5.32% / ≥5.32%
  - >1M: <1.70% / 1.70-4.52% / ≥4.52%

  — [HypeAuditor IG ER calculator](https://hypeauditor.com/free-tools/instagram-engagement-calculator/)
- HypeAuditor State of Influencer Marketing 2026 (via eMarketer excerpt): Instagram nano (1K-10K) ER **1.78%**, micro (10K-50K) **0.54%**, with an inverse relationship with follower count. HypeAuditor's 2025 blog average was 1.59% (<2% low, 2-3% good). TikTok "good" ER is around 10% as a rule of thumb — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/smaller-creators-deliver-efficiency-roi-pressure-mounts); [HypeAuditor TikTok ER](https://blog.hypeauditor.com/what-is-tiktok-engagement-rate-why-brands-should-take-note/) (search excerpts; PDF too large to fetch)
- Buffer (Sep 2025, 27M+ posts): Instagram median ER 4.3%; 4.6% for 1K-5K accounts (different methodology) — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-engagement-rate/)
- Dash Social H2 2025 (brand accounts): TikTok ~5.0%, Instagram ~3.6%, YouTube ~3.4%. H1 2025: 4.7% / 4.0% / 3.6%. ER = (likes + comments + shares) ÷ reach for TikTok/IG, ÷ video views for YouTube — [Dash Social via search](https://pages.dashsocial.com/hubfs/social-media-benchmark/2025/H2/all-industry.pdf)
- Favikon 2025 (6M+ profiles): Instagram nano top quartile ≥3.4%, mega top quartile ≤1.8%; TikTok mega rarely >1.5%; YouTube nano top quartile ≥0.8%, large creators ≥0.5%. The formula is not stated and the TikTok data looks unreliable ("800%+") — [Favikon](https://favikon.com/blog/favikon-engagement-benchmark-report-2025)
- India (upGrowth, Sep 2026): nano 6-12%; micro 3-7% on IG (4-9% on YT Shorts); mid 1.5-4%; macro 0.8-2.5% — [upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/influencer-marketing-pricing-india-2026/). Kofluence 2026: Tier 3/4 cities 4.5-5.5% ER — [Campaign India](https://www.campaignindia.in/article/kofluence-report-tracks-creator-economy-s-formal-shift/4xrn0g9cve1s8tcd4m0revck5k)
- Later 2026: typical ER 1-5%, CTR 0.5-2%, conversion 1-3% — [Later 2026](https://later.com/blog/influencer-pricing-benchmarks-the-complete-2026-guide/)
- Adjustments: Later gives no numeric multiplier. LeadDyno gives no multiplier but recommends cost per engagement = rate ÷ average engagements. Industry guides say higher ER justifies higher rates only if verified. One guide frames follower count as setting the range and ER as placing the creator high or low within it — [Later](https://later.com/blog/influencer-pricing-benchmarks-the-complete-2026-guide/); [LeadDyno](https://www.leaddyno.com/blog/influencer-pricing); [Hootsuite (search excerpt)](https://blog.hootsuite.com/influencer-pricing/)
- India: upGrowth uses cost per engaged view. A creator is competitive within 1.5x of paid CPVE, and overcharging starts at ≥3x — [upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/influencer-marketing-pricing-india-2026/)

### Inferences
- BVAL anchors on views, so compute ER by views ((likes + comments) ÷ views, per HypeAuditor) and compare it with the HypeAuditor tier bands. Suggested `er_adj`: below the "low" threshold → 0.8x; average band → 1.0x, interpolated linearly to 1.15x at the high threshold; above high → 1.25x, capped. Cap the premium because ER is easily gamed, and views already capture most of the reach value.
- My ±20-25% bounds are a design choice consistent with "ER places you within the tier range". They are not a published rule.
- Do not mix follower-based ER (around 0.5-2% on Instagram) with view-based ER (around 2-6%). Pick one, and use the matching benchmark table.

### Gaps
- No source publishes a numeric ER-to-price premium (e.g. "+X% per point above benchmark").
- No reliable 2025-2026 tier-level TikTok or YouTube ER table was retrieved (HypeAuditor's 2026 PDF exceeded the fetch size limit).

## 5. India: INR rate cards, CPMs, reports

### Takeaway
Indian Instagram Reel rates (2026) are roughly: nano ₹2K-12K, micro ₹8K-80K, mid ₹50K-3.5L, macro ₹2L-12L, mega ₹6L-25L+ per Reel. YouTube 60-90s integrations cost 2-4x a Reel (₹2-6L on a ~500K mid channel), dedicated videos ₹5-12L, and Shorts are priced near Reels. India's USD-equivalent is about 0.1-0.3x US rates. No source publishes India influencer CPMs.

### Cited Findings
- Grynow (21 Aug 2026) Reel rates: nano (1-10K) ₹1,000-12,000; micro (10-100K) ₹8,000-80,000; mid (100-500K) ₹50,000-3,50,000; macro (500K-2M) ₹2,00,000-12,00,000; mega (2M+) ₹6,00,000-25,00,000+. YouTube 60-90s integration on a mid-sized channel ~₹2-6 lakh; Shorts priced near Reels — [Grynow](https://www.grynow.in/blog/influencer-marketing-cost-in-india.html)

### Inferences
- Suggested India Reel base (INR, per Reel, geometric-ish midpoints of the Grynow and upGrowth ranges), with the USD equivalent at ₹93.5:

  | Tier | INR | USD | vs US config |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | nano | ₹5,000 | ~$53 | 0.21x |
  | micro (10-50K) | ₹25,000 | ~$267 | 0.30x |
  | mid (50-500K) | ₹1,50,000 | ~$1,604 | 0.46x |
  | macro (500K-1M) | ₹4,00,000 | ~$4,278 | 0.53x |
  | mega (1M+) | ₹10,00,000 | ~$10,695 | 0.53x |

- India discounts are steeper at nano/micro and shallower at the top. That broadly matches TrySpansa's 0.1-0.3x for the lower tiers. Model India either as a tier-dependent geo multiplier (nano 0.2 → mega 0.5) or as a separate INR table.
- India derived deliverables:
  - YouTube integration = 3x Reel (upGrowth 2-4x midpoint)
  - Dedicated = 2.5x integration (consistent with ₹5-12L vs ₹2.5-6L)
  - Shorts ≈ 1x Reel
  - Static ≈ 0.55x and Story ≈ 0.3x of Reel (US ratios; no India-specific numbers)
- Implied India Reel CPM (inference only): a micro creator with 30K median views at ₹25,000 → about ₹830 CPM (~$8.9). A view-based India CPM baseline of about ₹600-1,000 (lifestyle) is plausible, but it is not published.
